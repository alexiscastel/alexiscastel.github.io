This paper compares three approaches to launch vehicle ascent loading: a fitted Corcos turbulent boundary layer model, direct application of CFD fluctuating surface pressure, and generic surface pressure characterization in the wavenumber-frequency domain. Using a Saturn V Spacecraft-Lunar Module Adapter under transonic loading, the study examines which spatial and acoustic features each approach retains and how these choices affect the predicted structural and cavity response.
