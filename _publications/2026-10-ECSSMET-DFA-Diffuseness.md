This paper examines how acoustic field diffuseness affects the vibroacoustic response of an aluminum honeycomb test panel during Direct Field Acoustic testing. Numerical simulations of selected near-diffuse and non-diffuse test fields are compared with measured responses and experimental and finite element modal analyses. The study shows why matching overall sound pressure level and one-third-octave spectra alone is insufficient to ensure representative excitation of a test article’s structural modes.
